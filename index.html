Dia Dhuit! Thank you for visiting my website!

Dia Dhuit and welcome! I'm Feidhelm Joyce, born and raised in Ireland and currently living and working in Boston, MA. Since January 2026 I've been working at the Federal Home Loan Bank of Boston as a Business & Quality Assurance Analyst, and in May 2026 I completed my Master of Science in Applied Business Analytics at Boston University's Metropolitan College!

My journey to the US started in May 2022. Having just graduated from Dublin City University with a Bachelor's in Business & Management, I headed to the US with seven of my friends for the summer of a lifetime. All good things must come to an end, though, and that September I was fortunate enough to land a job on Allied Irish Banks' Financial Crime team in their Dublin office. Nine months later, in May 2023, I had the opportunity to take up a 14-month J1 work and travel visa to the United States. I seized it, and off I went!

Once there, I joined Emeraldly, a start-up based in Somerville, MA, that helps small and medium-sized businesses get set up on the CRM platform HubSpot. All of my previous experience had been in large organisations, so this was a brilliant introduction to what it means to work in a small company.

Working with Emeraldly also gave me a real insight into the power and value of data when it's leveraged correctly. I hadn't had the chance to specialise in anything like this during my undergrad, which got me thinking about next steps. I was lucky enough to be accepted into the Applied Business Analytics program at BU, and after a challenging, fast-paced 16 months, I graduated in May 2026 with a 3.9 GPA and plenty of lessons to take into my career, from both inside and outside the classroom.

Before my time in the US, I grew up on the northside of Dublin, then moved to Blackrock, Co. Louth, when I was 11. It was there that I really developed my love for all things sport, and I was lucky to be part of successful hurling, Gaelic football, soccer and golf teams, picking up some silverware along the way. I'm happy to say that love hasn't faded since moving to the States!

This website is a small insight into my experiences so far, from a professional, academic and personal perspective. Hope you enjoy!
